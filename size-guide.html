How to Measure

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal.

Length

Measure from the highest point of the shoulder to the desired hem length.

Sleeve

Measure from shoulder seam to cuff with arm slightly bent.

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, keeping the tape comfortably loose.

Fit Tips

Regular Fit

Our standard fit with comfortable room for movement. Perfect for everyday wear.

Slim Fit

Closer to the body with a modern silhouette. Consider sizing up if you prefer more room.

Oversized

Intentionally loose and relaxed. True to size for the intended oversized look.
