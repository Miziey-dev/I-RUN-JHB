Returns & Exchanges

30-Day Return Policy

We want you to love your I RUN JHB purchase. If you're not completely satisfied, you can return items within 30 days of delivery.

Return Conditions

  • Items must be unworn and in original condition
  • All original tags must be attached
  • Items must be in original packaging
  • Proof of purchase required
  • No signs of wear, damage, or alteration

Non-Returnable Items

  • Underwear and intimate apparel
  • Customized or personalized items
  • Items damaged by misuse
  • Items without original tags

How to Return

  1. Contact us at returns@irunjhb.com or +27 11 123 4567
  2. Provide your order number and reason for return
  3. Receive return authorization and instructions
  4. Package items securely with return form
  5. Ship to our returns address

Exchange Policy

We offer exchanges for:

  • Different sizes (subject to availability)
  • Different colors (subject to availability)
  • Defective items

Exchange shipping is free for defective items. Customer pays shipping for size/color exchanges.

Refund Process

  • Refunds processed within 5-10 business days
  • Refund to original payment method
  • Customer responsible for return shipping costs
  • Original shipping costs non-refundable

Defective Items

If you receive a defective item:

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery
  • We'll provide a prepaid return label
  • Full refund or replacement provided
  • No return shipping charges
