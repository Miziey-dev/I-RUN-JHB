Frequently Asked Questions

Orders & Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayFast for instant EFT, and cash on delivery for select areas.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with tracking information. You can also track your order on our Track Your Order page.

Can I modify or cancel my order?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. Contact us immediately at +27 11 123 4567 or orders@irunjhb.com.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days, express shipping takes 1-2 business days. Free shipping is available on orders over R500.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes, we ship to all provinces in South Africa. Delivery times may vary for remote areas.

What if I'm not home for delivery?

The courier will leave a delivery card. You can arrange redelivery or collect from the nearest depot within 7 days.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy. Items must be unworn, with original tags, and in original packaging. See our Returns & Exchanges page for details.

How do I exchange for a different size?

Contact our returns team at returns@irunjhb.com with your order number. We'll guide you through the exchange process.

Who pays for return shipping?

Customers pay return shipping costs unless the item is defective or we made an error.

Sizing & Fit

How do I find my size?

Use our size guide available on each product page. Measure yourself and compare to our size chart for the best fit.

Do your items run true to size?

Our items generally run true to size. Check individual product descriptions for specific fit notes.

What if the size doesn't fit?

You can exchange for a different size within 30 days, subject to availability. See our exchange policy for details.

Products & Care

How should I care for my I RUN JHB items?

Follow the care instructions on each garment's label. Visit our Care Instructions page for detailed guidelines.

Are your products ethically made?

Yes, we work with suppliers who meet our ethical standards for fair labor practices and sustainable production.

When will out-of-stock items be available?

Restock dates vary by item. Sign up for notifications on product pages to be alerted when items are back in stock.

Account & Website

Do I need an account to place an order?

No, you can checkout as a guest. However, creating an account allows you to track orders and save preferences.

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
