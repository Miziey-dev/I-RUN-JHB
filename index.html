The I RUN JHB Story

Born in the City of Gold, I RUN JHB is more than a brand—it's a movement. We are a premium South African street culture lifestyle brand that fuses South African traditional culture, Kasi (township) trends and African traditional culture aesthetics to create Urban streetwear called UMSWENKO. Umswenko is an authentic South African street sub-culture and an expression of dressing well.

The name celebrates the iconic world-class city of Johannesburg, synonymous with bright city lights where ambitious beings come together to hustle and make their dreams a reality. Our garments are the epitome of Johannesburg as a city that accommodates descendants from different parts of Africa.

South African Traditional Culture

We create traditional culture wear for different cultures and ethnic groups in South Africa using indigenous traditional culture fabrics unique to our heritage. These include IsiZulu, IsiXhosa, IsiSwati, IsiNdebele, Sepedi, Sesotho, Setswana, Xitsonga and TshiVenda cultures.

Kasi (Township) Street Culture

We create kasi street culture wear through carefully selecting and creating fabrics synonymous with township trends and street sub-cultures like amapantsula, matariana and izikhothane found in different South African townships.

African Traditional Aesthetics

We use ethnic African patterns to create African traditional culture inspired streetwear that celebrates our rich continental heritage and connects with youth aged 18-35 years across Africa and internationally.
