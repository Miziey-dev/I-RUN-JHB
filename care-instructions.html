Care Instructions

Keep your I RUN JHB streetwear looking fresh with proper care.

General Care Guidelines

  • Always check the care label before washing
  • Separate colors to prevent bleeding
  • Turn garments inside out before washing
  • Use cold water to preserve colors and prevent shrinking
  • Avoid overloading the washing machine

Cotton & Cotton Blends

  • Machine wash cold (30°C or below)
  • Use mild detergent
  • Tumble dry low or air dry
  • Iron on medium heat if needed
  • Avoid bleach and fabric softeners

Hoodies & Sweatshirts

  • Wash inside out to protect prints
  • Use cold water and gentle cycle
  • Air dry to maintain shape
  • If using dryer, use low heat
  • Store on hangers to prevent stretching

Printed & Graphic Tees

  • Always wash inside out
  • Cold water only
  • Gentle or delicate cycle
  • Air dry recommended
  • Iron on reverse side only
  • Avoid direct heat on prints

Denim & Heavy Fabrics

  • Wash separately first few times
  • Cold water to prevent fading
  • Turn inside out
  • Air dry when possible
  • Iron on medium-high heat

Storage Tips

  • Fold heavy items like hoodies
  • Hang delicate items
  • Store in cool, dry place
  • Avoid direct sunlight
  • Use cedar blocks to prevent moths

Stain Removal

  • Treat stains immediately
  • Blot, don't rub stains
  • Use cold water for blood and sweat
  • Pre-treat with stain remover
  • Test cleaning products on hidden area first

What to Avoid

  • Hot water (causes shrinking and fading)
  • Bleach (damages fabric and prints)
  • High heat drying
  • Dry cleaning (unless specified)
  • Ironing directly on prints
  • Fabric softeners on performance fabrics
