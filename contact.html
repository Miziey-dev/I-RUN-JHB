Frequently Asked Questions

What is UMSWENKO?

UMSWENKO is our signature streetwear philosophy that fuses traditional South African culture with contemporary Kasi (township) trends and modern urban aesthetics.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes, we ship across South Africa. Free shipping is available on orders over R500. Same-day delivery is available in Johannesburg.

What's your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unworn items in original condition. Visit our store or contact us for return assistance.

Do you offer custom designs?

We offer limited custom design services for special orders. Contact us to discuss your requirements and minimum quantities.

How can I become a stockist?

We're always looking for premium retail partners. Visit our Stockists page or contact partners@irunjozi.co.za for partnership opportunities.

Are your products ethically made?

Yes, we're committed to ethical production and supporting local South African communities through our manufacturing partnerships.
