Our Story

The journey of UMSWENKO and South African street culture

Born in the City of Gold

I RUN JHB is more than a brand—it's a movement celebrating Johannesburg's hustle culture and African heritage through premium streetwear called UMSWENKO.

Our Genesis

Founded in the heart of Johannesburg, I RUN JHB emerged from a deep passion for South African street culture and the vibrant energy of the City of Gold. We recognized the need for authentic streetwear that truly represents the spirit of Johannesburg's youth and their relentless hustle.

The UMSWENKO Philosophy

UMSWENKO is more than just clothing—it's a lifestyle that fuses traditional African culture with contemporary Kasi (township) trends. Our designs celebrate the rich tapestry of South African heritage while embracing the modern urban aesthetic that defines Johannesburg's street culture.

Our Mission

To create premium streetwear that authentically represents South African street culture, empowering youth aged 18-35 to express their identity through fashion that celebrates both tradition and innovation.

Our Vision

To become the leading South African streetwear brand that bridges the gap between African heritage and contemporary urban fashion, inspiring a global appreciation for Johannesburg's unique street culture.

Our Values

Authenticity, cultural pride, quality craftsmanship, and community empowerment drive everything we do. We believe in celebrating our roots while pushing boundaries in streetwear design.

Who We Serve

African Youth (18-35)

Young South Africans who are proud of their heritage and want to express their cultural identity through contemporary streetwear that speaks to their urban lifestyle.

Cultural Tourists

Visitors to South Africa seeking authentic, locally-made fashion that captures the essence of Johannesburg's vibrant street culture and African heritage.

International Audience

Global streetwear enthusiasts who appreciate unique, culturally-rich designs and want to connect with authentic African urban fashion.

What Drives Us

Authenticity

Every piece we create is rooted in genuine South African street culture, ensuring our designs remain true to their origins while pushing creative boundaries.

Quality Craftsmanship

We use only premium materials and employ skilled artisans to ensure every garment meets our high standards for durability, comfort, and style.
